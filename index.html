Privacy Policy

Contacts Only | Effective November 5, 2026

Contacts Only is a single-purpose Android application that automatically blocks incoming calls from numbers that are not saved in your contacts.

The application operates entirely on your device. It does not collect, transmit, or share personal information.

How It Works

Contacts Only uses Android's built-in call screening system. Android determines whether an incoming caller is in your contacts. Calls from saved contacts are not passed to Contacts Only.

For other incoming calls, the application checks whether call blocking is enabled. If enabled, it instructs Android to reject the call.

Contacts Only never reads phone numbers, caller names, or your contact list.

Data Collection and Sharing

Contacts Only does not collect or share personal information. Specifically, the application:

  • Does not access your contacts or call history.
  • Does not read or record phone numbers.
  • Does not access the internet.
  • Does not contain advertising or analytics.
  • Does not track users.
  • Does not require accounts or registration.
  • Does not transmit data to third parties.

Local Storage

The application stores only your on/off preference, locally on your device.

This setting is excluded from Android cloud backups and device-to-device transfers.

Contacts Only does not maintain a database of contacts, callers, or blocked calls.

Android may independently record blocked calls in your device's call history as part of its normal operation.

Android Call Screening Authorization

When you enable the switch in Contacts Only, the application initiates Android's built-in process for selecting it as your call screening application.

Android manages this authorization. You can revoke it through your device's settings or disable call blocking using the application's switch.

Contacts Only requests no Android permissions.

Children's Privacy

Contacts Only is suitable for users of all ages, including children.

The application does not collect personal information from children or adults. It does not display advertisements, track users, or provide social features.

The same privacy protections apply to every user, regardless of age.

Data Retention and Deletion

The developer does not receive or retain user information.

You can delete the locally stored on/off preference by clearing the application's storage or uninstalling the application.
