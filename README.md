# Privacy Policy — Contacts Only

**Effective Date:** November 5, 2026

Contacts Only is a free, single-purpose Android application that automatically blocks incoming calls from numbers that are not saved in your contacts.

The application operates entirely on your device and does not collect, transmit, or share personal information.

## How It Works

Contacts Only uses Android's built-in call screening system.

Android determines whether an incoming caller is in your contacts. Calls from saved contacts are not passed to Contacts Only.

For other incoming calls, the application checks whether call blocking is enabled and, if so, instructs Android to reject the call.

The application never reads phone numbers, caller names, or your contact list.

## Data Collection and Sharing

Contacts Only does not collect or share any personal information.

Specifically:

- No access to your contacts or call history.
- No reading or recording of phone numbers.
- No internet access or network communication.
- No advertising, analytics, or tracking.
- No user accounts or registration.
- No transmission of information to the developer or third parties.

## Local Storage

The application stores only your on/off preference, locally on your device.

This preference is excluded from Android cloud backups and device-to-device transfers.

The application does not maintain a database of contacts, callers, or blocked calls.

Android may independently record blocked calls in your device's call history as part of its normal operation.

## Android Call Screening Authorization

When you enable the switch in Contacts Only, the application initiates Android's built-in process for selecting it as your call screening application.

Android manages this authorization. You can revoke it at any time through your device's settings or disable call blocking using the application's switch.

Contacts Only requests no Android permissions.

## Children's Privacy

Contacts Only is suitable for users of all ages, including children.

The application does not collect personal information from children or adults, display advertisements, or provide social or communication features.

The same privacy protections apply to every user, regardless of age.

## Data Retention and Deletion

The developer does not receive or retain user information.

The locally stored on/off preference can be deleted by clearing the application's storage or uninstalling the application.
